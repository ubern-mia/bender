# About the series

**BENDER** — **BE**st practices in medical imagi**N**g **DE**ep lea**R**ning — is a series of
short videos and companion material made by the
[Medical Image Analysis group](https://www.artorg.unibe.ch/research/mia/) at the
[ARTORG Center](https://www.artorg.unibe.ch), University of Bern.

The first four episodes were made for the
[MICCAI Education Challenge 2022](https://miccai-sb.github.io/challenge.html), and the
series has kept going since — roughly one topic per season.

## What it is for

Most deep learning tutorials teach you to build a model. Rather fewer teach you the habits
that decide whether the model is worth anything: looking at the data first, changing one
thing at a time, reporting per-class metrics, comparing against a baseline you did not
choose to flatter yourself, and being honest about what a number means.

The series follows a new student — Satish — through a project, so those habits arrive as
mistakes rather than as advice. That turns out to be much easier to remember.

## The format

Each episode is a short animated film, generally under 20 minutes, with characters who
argue with each other. The companion material in this repository is the part you can run:
notebooks, training scripts, a federated simulation, a checklist and a glossary.

The series has run since August 2022:

| Season | Episodes | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (2022) | 1–4 | One project, start to finish — the MICCAI Education Challenge entry |
| 2 (2022–23) | 5–6 | Interpretability, and the U-Net |
| 3 (2024) | 7–8 | Generative models, foundation models |
| 4 (2025) | 9 | Federated learning |
